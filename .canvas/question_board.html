CSC-225 Advanced Python Programming

Ask Questions

Programming is new for many of us, and questions are a normal part of learning. Use this forum to ask as many questions as you need throughout the course.

Your questions may be about an error in your code, help understanding an assignment, or clarification about a topic from the notebooks, videos, or class materials.

Participation Requirement

  • To receive full participation credit, you are expected to ask at least two questions related to the course subject matter during the semester.
  • Good questions can include code errors, debugging steps, confusing concepts, assignment requirements, or connections between course topics.
