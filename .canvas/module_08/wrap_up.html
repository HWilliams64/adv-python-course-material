Module 08: Wrapping-up and Looking Ahead

CSC-225 Advanced Python Programming

Wrapping Up

  • This module explored AI-assisted programming, prompting, code explanation, debugging support, agents, and careful review of generated code.
  • You practiced treating AI as a tool that can help you think, revise, and test, not as a replacement for understanding your program.
  • Review examples where testing or explanation exposed a mistake, because verification is the key habit in AI-supported work.

Looking Ahead

  • This is the final module, so the next step is to bring together the course skills in your final work and future programming projects.
  • Use the habits from the course: break problems into parts, choose appropriate tools, test your code, and explain your decisions clearly.
